What this is

This page maps burn incidents across Pakistan alongside the country's specialized burn treatment centers, so you can see where care is close by and where access gaps exist.

Data in this version

The incident points plotted here are illustrative sample data, not a live feed of real cases — building a genuine live version needs a real incident-reporting pipeline and hospital dataset behind it. The burn-center locations are approximate positions of well-known major facilities and should be verified before being relied on for anything operational.

Methodology

Access distance is calculated as straight-line (haversine) distance from each incident to the nearest listed burn center, grouped into four bands: under 25 km, 25–50 km, 50–100 km, and over 100 km. Real road-network travel time would be more accurate than straight-line distance.
